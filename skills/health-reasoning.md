# Health Reasoning Skill

## When to load

Load when reasoning about scoring formulas, interpreting trends,
ranking interventions, or evaluating divergences between predicted
and felt scores.

## Section 1: Inflammatory + autonomic fusion (NLR × HRV)

[All your literature here — Forget 2017, Lee/Sennels/Berg 2021,
Walsh/Gleeson, the Sternal/Kalinkovich sympathetic-parasympathetic link,
Aeschbacher 2017, the COVID-HRV-NLR correlation work]

Key mechanism: neutrophils ↔ sympathetic, lymphocytes ↔ parasympathetic.
This is why HRV (parasympathetic readout) can serve as a non-invasive
proxy for NLR normalization between blood draws.

Diagnostic patterns:

- HRV improving + NLR still elevated = autonomic recovery preceding
  inflammatory resolution. Common post-illness. Premature reload risk.
- HRV declining + NLR elevated = active stressor, both systems strained.
- HRV stable + NLR normalizing = full recovery trajectory.

## Section 4: How these three integrate

The three metrics view the body through different lenses:

- NLR×HRV = inflammation + autonomic state (systemic)
- SRI = circadian alignment (chronobiological)
- Pa:HR = exercise-economy drift (peripheral + thermoregulatory)

Convergent signals (all three degrading) = strong reload-risk signal.
Divergent signals = diagnostic — see Section 1 patterns.

healthOS

Personal health intelligence layer — ingests messy multi-source health data (Zepp/Amazfit, JeFit, Strava, blood panels) and produces monthly-style reports with a composite readiness score, bio-age proxy, and ranked interventions when you run the full local pipeline.

Two ways to use it

Here (browser, no install): walk through gathering data, copy prompts for your own LLM, and paste a CSV for a quick qualitative read. No data is sent to healthOS; anything you paste goes only where you paste it.

Locally (full pipeline): clone the repo, run the Python ingest + scorers and the Vite dashboard — that is where NLR×HRV, SRI, aerobic decoupling, composite, bio-age, and intervention ranking are computed from transparent formulas.

The wedge

No single consumer app sees all of this at once. healthOS is built to fuse wearables, workouts, and blood differentials into one calendar so you can reason about recovery and load together — not as medical advice, but as a structured personal report.

Three flagship metrics (local pipeline)

  • NLR × HRV training-readiness (labs + autonomic)
  • Sleep Regularity Index (Phillips-style consistency)
  • Aerobic decoupling trend (pace/HR)
